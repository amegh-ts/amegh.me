The short version

Full-stackDatabase to interface

Full-stack, for me, means owning a feature from the table it's stored in to the button someone presses: the schema, the API, the screen and the tests that keep it working.

Most of what I've built so far are products where people book something and pay for it: counselling sessions at Oppam, tour packages and holidays at Club Travalet. That work has made me careful about three things that are easy to get wrong: money, time and data.

Day to day I work in Next.js and Node.js, with PostgreSQL or MongoDB underneath.

What I do now

Since Dec 2025Oppam, Kozhikode

I'm a Software Developer at Oppam, an online counselling platform. Clients use it to find a counsellor, book a session and pay; the Oppam team runs everything else from an internal admin panel.

I work on both sides of that. On the client side, payments and pricing. On the team side, HR tools, permissions and the admin screens. Across the platform, I led an audit of how dates and times are handled, and I write end-to-end tests for the flows that matter most.
