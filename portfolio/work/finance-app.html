Finance App

Overview

An expense tracker for web, with an Android companion. Bank SMS messages are read on the phone and turned into transactions automatically.

Type

  • Personal project

Platforms

  • Web, PWA
  • Android

Written by

  • Amegh
  • 14 Nov 2026

The problem

WhyManual logging stops

Logging every card spend and EMI payment by hand is slow, and most people stop doing it. The data is already arriving on the phone as bank SMS messages and notifications, so the app reads it from there.

How it works

ArchitecturePhone, workflow, web app
  • Android companion. A Flutter and Kotlin app captures bank SMS messages and notifications and filters them on the device.
  • n8n import workflow. Parses card spends and EMI payments into transactions. Anything it can't match goes to a review queue instead of being guessed.
  • Live web app. Balances update over Socket.IO, web push alerts fire for budgets and due dates, and it installs as a PWA.

Features

In the appMoney, at a glance
  • Budgets with alert thresholds
  • Loan repayment history
  • Savings goals
  • Six-month analytics view
