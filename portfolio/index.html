Personal project

Finance OS

Expense tracker for web, with an Android companion

Tracks spending, budgets, loans and savings goals. Bank SMS messages are read on the phone and turned into transactions automatically.

  • Bank SMS auto-import: an n8n workflow parses card spends and EMI payments, and a review queue catches anything it can't match.
  • Android companion in Flutter and Kotlin captures bank SMS and notifications and filters them on the device.
  • Live balances over Socket.IO, web push alerts for budgets and due dates, installable as a PWA.
  • Budgets with alert thresholds, loan repayment history and a six-month analytics view.
